The pricing problem

Most merchants are leaving money on every sale, with no way to know how much.

Pricing feels like a guessing game because it is. Without a systematic way to read your own sales data, you're choosing numbers in the dark.

Gut-feel pricing
You pick a number that feels right and hope customers agree. But is $79 better than $89? You genuinely can't tell without data, and data takes time you don't have.
Copying competitors
Your competitors sell to different customers in different markets. Matching their price assumes your buyers are identical to theirs. They're not, and you're leaving margin on the table.
Set it and forget it
You updated your prices months ago and haven't touched them since. Meanwhile your costs shifted, your product mix changed, and the right price moved with it.
The Zorin solution
The answer is already in your order history — you've never had a tool to read it.

Every sale you've ever made is a data point. When 100 customers bought at $49 but only 55 bought when you raised it to $59, that gap tells you exactly how price-sensitive your buyers are.

Zorin reads thousands of those signals across your entire catalog, calculates the price that maximizes profit for each product, and surfaces a raise, lower, or hold recommendation — without a spreadsheet or a statistics background.

"I'll try $79 and see what happens."
→ "My elasticity is –1.2. Raising to $85 lifts profit 14%."
"My competitor charges $89 so I'll charge $89."
→ "My customers are less price-sensitive. I can charge $97."
"I haven't touched prices in six months."
→ "Three products are under-priced. Zorin flagged them."
