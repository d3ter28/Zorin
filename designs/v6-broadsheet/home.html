Prestige pricing: when a higher price sells more

Buyers use price as evidence of quality. What the research shows, a worked example, and the categories where it quietly fails.

By Dexter · 19 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

Prestige pricing means deliberately setting a high price to signal quality, exclusivity or status. For some products a higher price doesn't just survive, it makes the product more desirable. In a 2008 PNAS study, the same wine was rated more pleasant when labeled at a higher price.
