# Product

## Product Name

Zorin

## Product Purpose

Zorin is an ML-powered pricing intelligence platform for online merchants. It measures price elasticity for every SKU from sales history, generates profit-optimising price recommendations, and provides a Launch Planner for planning promotional campaigns with scenario modelling. The platform turns pricing from guesswork into a continuous, data-backed decision loop. Success means merchants price at the right level, not the habitual one.

## Users

Small to mid-size e-commerce operators (Shopify, WooCommerce) who set prices by gut feel or competitor-watching. They are business-minded, not data scientists. They need a number they can act on — not a model to configure. They are time-poor and trust proof over promise.

## Positioning

Priced below enterprise analytics tools; aimed at merchants who cannot afford an in-house analyst but whose margins are material enough that a 10–15% pricing improvement changes the month. The core differentiator: elasticity modelling that runs automatically from uploaded sales history, with no model tuning required.

## Brand Personality

Precise. Trustworthy. Clear.

Not a hype machine. Not startup-chirpy. The tone is confident, specific, and commercial — like a sharp analyst who explains the number and tells you what to do with it. Data leads; prose follows.

## Operating Context

Browser-first SaaS. Dashboard used from a laptop, typically in a back-office or home-office setting, during business hours. Marketing site discovered via search or referral. Early-access stage: the brand must earn trust before the product has a long track record.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers first.** Every claim is grounded in a figure. The interface leads with data.
2. **Clarity over cleverness.** Merchants making pricing decisions need unambiguous signals.
3. **Earned trust.** Confidence comes from specificity and visible mechanism, not from aesthetic warmth.
4. **Continuous intelligence.** The value is the ongoing loop, not a one-off report.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS blue + rounded card + pastel gradient (the modal B2B default)
- Enterprise dashboard overload (too many metrics, no clear hierarchy)
- Startup-cheerful (emoji-heavy, VC-speak, "supercharge your growth")
- Academic data-viz (dense tables, chart-heavy, no synthesis)
